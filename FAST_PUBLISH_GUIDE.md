## 🚀 Option 1: CERN Zenodo (Instant Official DOI in 5 Minutes — 100% Free)

**Zenodo** is operated by CERN (the European Organization for Nuclear Research) and OpenAIRE. It issues an official, permanent, globally recognized **DOI** (`10.5281/zenodo.xxxxxx`) immediately upon upload and is indexed on Google Scholar.

### Steps to Publish on Zenodo:
1. Go to: **https://zenodo.org/**
2. Click **Log in** (or **Sign Up** using your GitHub / ORCID / Email).
3. Click the green **+ New Upload** button at the top right.
4. **Drag & Drop** your file: `Clean_EpigraphiX_AI_Research_Paper.pdf`.
5. Fill in the metadata:
   - **Resource type**: `Publication` -> `Preprint` (or `Working paper`)
   - **Title**: `EpigraphiX-AI: Neural Epigraphical OCR, Topological Binarization, and Multi-Model Intelligence for Degraded Historical Palm-Leaf Manuscripts`
   - **Authors**: `Adarsh S.` (Affiliation: `APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University`)
   - **Description**: Copy and paste the Abstract from Section 2 of your paper.
   - **Keywords**: `Palm-Leaf Manuscripts`, `Epigraphical OCR`, `Digital Palaeography`, `Sauvola Binarization`, `Support Vector Machines`, `Grantha Script`, `Sandhi Grammar`
   - **License**: `Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)`
6. Click **Publish** at the bottom.
7. **Done!** You will immediately receive:
   - An immutable official **DOI** (e.g. `https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.XXXXXXX`).
   - A permanent CERN-hosted web page for your paper.
   - Automatic indexing into Google Scholar.
